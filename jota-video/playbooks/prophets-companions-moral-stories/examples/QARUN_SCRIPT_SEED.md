# Example Narrative Seed (PLAN ONLY) — Qarun and the Limits of Wealth

**Working title:** *“Qarun: Harta yang Tidak Menyelamatkan”*. Category: `QURANIC_OPPRESSOR`. No figures representing Musa (a prophet). If Qarun or unidentified people are shown, entire faces must be completely featureless on all frames; safer scene plan uses objects and architectural environment.

## Four-beat creative structure (no actual generated media)
1. **Hook:** A pile of ornaments and key-shaped objects on a table; narrator asks “Apa yang sebenarnya membuat seseorang kaya?” *Reflective question is JOTA original prose, not a verse or hadith.*
2. **Source-based context:** Abstract weight of wealth and extraordinary burden, close-up of counting objects, without showing Musa or pretending the objects are archaeological artifacts from Qarun's time.
3. **Outcome:** Neutral illustrated landscape changes, scripture reference to Al-Qasas 28:81 appears as deterministic text overlay; no fake eyewitness footage or dramatic invented violence, and no attempt to visualize Allah.
4. **Ibrah:** Quiet environment, narration that treasures cannot replace humility and gratitude, clearly a contemporary moral reflection grounded in the cited passage.

## Required gates before release
- Review complete Al-Qasas passage/context with proficient religious reviewer; no unverified dialogue, dates, architecture, location or supernatural spectacle.
- If Qur'anic verse is onscreen, quote exact script+edition and human-check diacritics/translation/voice. No background music and all living subjects featureless.
- If there is an ordinary human narrator/presenter animation, keep it generic—not Musa, not a sahabi impersonation—and faceless. Narration need not show a speaking avatar.
- No rights-cleared photo/voice, no character asset, no recorded audio or video has been created by this planning document.
