# JOTA — Kisah & Ibrah

**Original independent skill/playbook v1.4.0 — PLAN ONLY / no video created.**

A history-and-ethics video track for:
1. **Kisah para nabi dan rasul:** only what authentic Qur'an, hadith and reviewed tafsir support.
2. **Kisah sahabat Rasulullah ﷺ:** credible sahih hadith and reviewed sirah/history; identify conflicting reports rather than claiming certainty.
3. **Kisah orang zalim dan kaum terdahulu:** e.g. Fir'aun, Qarun, 'Ad, Thamud when supported by precise scriptures; teach consequences and human lessons without invented graphic depictions.
4. **Kisah teladan umum:** historically sourced scholars/people or clearly labeled *original fiction inspired by Islamic values*; never falsely attribute fiction to a prophet/sahabi.

## Brand and production standards
Indonesian-language cinematic original 2D storybook, period-aware objects/settings, substantial motion, natural ambient sounds, no music, no lip sync. **Absolutely no personification of prophets or angels, even blank-faced silhouette.** Named companions are not directly depicted by default pending owner/religious review. Any other visible human/animal characters must be 100% smooth faceless.
