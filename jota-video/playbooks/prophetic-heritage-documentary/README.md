# JOTA — Jejak Peradaban Para Nabi
**Original documentary video-agent playbook; specification v1.4.0 — NOT ENABLED**

**Purpose:** investigate historic and archaeological places mentioned in or associated with the stories of prophets, and explain what the religious texts actually say versus what archaeology can substantiate. Primarily Indonesian narration, cinematic site photography/rights-cleared footage, original illustrated maps, object-level animations and reconstruction of environments — **never personify prophets**.

## Pilot categories (ideas only)
1. Al-Hijr/Hegra: Quranic residents and later extant Nabataean architecture; never assert the monumental tombs belong to the people of Salih.
2. Maqam Ibrahim: traditional/religious meaning and material-claim caution; evidence check before any specific claim.
3. Sites linked to Musa: multiple competing location traditions; do not choose a modern mountain/site as certain without source review.
4. Historical landscapes, routes, wells and buildings associated with early Islamic narratives: label reliability tier and period.

**No new media generation or external-site visit is authorized by storing this playbook.**
