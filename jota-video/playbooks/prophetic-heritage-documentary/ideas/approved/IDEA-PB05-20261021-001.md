## Planning brief

- **Main question:** “Apa yang Al-Qur'an ceritakan tentang Al-Hijr, dan apa yang benar-benar diketahui tentang bangunan batu di Hegra yang dapat kita lihat sekarang?”
- **Episode angle:** Explore *two separate histories*: scriptural accounts associated with the people of Al-Hijr / Prophet Salih and independently examined archaeological heritage at present-day Hegra. The word “misteri” is a curiosity hook, **not** a claim of supernatural/hidden discoveries.
- **Audience:** Indonesian Muslim family viewers, general history-curious audience (age suitability to be reviewed).
- **Proposed form:** Short documentary in Indonesian with warm, original 2D architectural reconstructions, licensed authentic site images only where usage rights are documented, maps and animated timelines. Suggested planning length **90–180 seconds**, adjustable by owner after script and evidence research; ratio TBD (9:16 for Shorts vs 16:9 for wider documentary).
- **Takeaway / ibrah:** Reflect on humility, the consequences of rejecting guidance as supported by scripture, and the importance of distinguishing religious narration from physical evidence. Do not claim a particular rock tomb was built by Salih or Thamud.
