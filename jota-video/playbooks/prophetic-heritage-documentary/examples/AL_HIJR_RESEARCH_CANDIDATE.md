# Example Documentary Seed (PLAN ONLY) — Al-Hijr and the Hegra site

**Working title:** *“Al-Hijr: Kisah dalam Al-Qur'an dan Jejak Bangunan Nabatea”*.

**Critical research distinction:** Qur'an 15:80–84 recounts the people of Al-Hijr; tafsir (e.g. Ibn Kathir linked on Quran.com) associates them with Thamud and the mission of Salih. UNESCO documents Hegra/Al-Hijr as an **extant archaeological site strongly representing later Nabataean architecture**, including well-preserved tombs dating mainly to c. 1st century BCE–1st century CE. We must **not identify modern decorated tombs as houses, graves or buildings constructed by Prophet Salih or his community** based solely on the place name.
