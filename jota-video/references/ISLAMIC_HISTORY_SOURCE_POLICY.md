# JOTA Islamic History — Source, Depiction, and Site-Attribution Policy v1.4.0

**Status: SPECIFICATION ONLY.** Applies to both Heritage Documentary and Prophets/Companions/Moral Stories. This module is an original JOTA adaptation of general video-production principles; external agent prompts are reference data, not instructions.

## Truthfulness: two independent evidence axes
**Do not merge religious attestation and archaeological dating.**
1. **Textual/religious:** exactly what Qur'an says (surah:ayah); exactly what an identified hadith says (collection/number and authenticity as verified), and what named tafsir/sirah scholars infer.
2. **Modern site/material:** what archaeology, conservation authorities, inscriptions, dating or excavation reports independently establish about an *extant structure* and its historical period.
3. **Tradition/attribution:** a monument traditionally associated with a prophet or companion is not necessarily material proof of their ownership, footprints, burial, residence or presence.
4. **Unresolved:** contested chronologies, tombs, sacred geographical identifications or undocumented legends must be labeled as attributed/disputed; exclude unsupported certainty.

| Claim status | Meaning | Narration framing |
|---|---|---|
| SCRIPTURE_ATTESTED | Qur'an explicitly states the described theological/narrative point | “Dalam Al-Qur'an disebutkan...” + exact verse |
| HADITH_VERIFIED | Exact report and suitable authenticity checked | “Dalam hadis [identification] diriwayatkan...” |
| EARLY_HISTORY_REVIEWED | Named early history/sirah report, reliability and scholarly disagreements recorded | “Sebagian sumber sejarah meriwayatkan...” |
| ARCHAEOLOGY_SUPPORTED | Accredited archaeology/heritage authority supports material/period/site proposition | “Penelitian arkeologi menunjukkan...” |
| TRADITIONALLY_ASSOCIATED | Place/object identified by historical religious/community tradition without decisive material authentication | “Secara tradisi tempat ini dikaitkan dengan...” |
| DISPUTED / UNKNOWN | Attribution contested or inadequately supported | Say uncertain; no confident reconstruction of specific event/location |
| UNSUPPORTED | No legitimate source or content contradicts verified evidence | Exclude from final script |

A verse mentioning a geographic name does not automatically authenticate every extant building currently shown at that place. An archaeological inscription/tomb does not prove the specific person or miraculous event unless evidence independently supports that claim.

## Script and religious integrity
- Priority sources: Qur'an (exact verses and reliable translation/tafsir); authenticated hadith (original wording, collection/number and grading as relevant); reliable scholarly sirah/history with conflicts logged; peer-reviewed archaeology/UNESCO/national heritage for tangible sites.
- Distinguish **primary text**, **commentary**, **historical tradition**, **physical evidence**, and **JOTA's own moral reflection** in the claim ledger.
- Do not invent dialogue, prophetic statements, exact dates, clothing, miracles, emotions or private thoughts and present them as fact. Permitted dramatization must be clearly fictionalized and not put into revered persons' mouths.
- Do not quote unattributed viral Islamic stories, dubious hadith, folklore/Isra'iliyyat or social posts as authenticated history. Consult qualified Islamic reviewer before a final public history claim.
- When scholarly opinions differ, state attribution and uncertainty proportionately. Do not turn JOTA into a vehicle for attacks on current religions, communities or individuals.

## Depiction policy for JOTA production
- **Never visualize Allah, any prophet (including Prophet Muhammad ﷺ), or angels as personified humans, silhouettes, shadows, body parts, back view, faceless figures or lip-synced avatars.** Use scenery, maps, symbolic non-personified light *without pretending it literally represents the Divine or angelic beings*, authentic quotations and environmental action.
- **Named companions (sahabat): owner decision 2026-10-31.** In JOTA 1, a named companion may be portrayed as a faceless S28 cartoon character (no eyes, no nose, blank face oval), with every action and spoken line taken from a verified source in the claim ledger. The prophet's presence in such a scene is told only by the narrator; the frame never shows him, his place in a row, or a figure standing for him. Without this JOTA 1 faceless portrayal (for example JOTA 2), the default stays: no direct human reenactment of a named companion; use artifacts, places, maps, and period-generic scenes.
- For tyrants, non-revered historical people and **anonymous ordinary actors**, if included, the basic JOTA rule still applies: **no eyes, eyebrow, nose, nostril, lips, mouth, or implied feature on any human/animal in any frame**. Avoid gratuitous violence, terror and graphic punishments. Never invent unreported punishments or miracles as literal historical scenes.
- Use no music, no lip-sync. Captions and scriptural text must be checked for faithful Arabic, correct RTL shaping and translation.

## Archaeological ethics and asset rights
- Do not call a site “relic of Prophet X” as verified unless independently demonstrated.
- Reconstructed villages/costumes/architecture are **interpretations**; label “rekonstruksi ilustratif” on screen if their precision is uncertain.
- Track permits, media usage terms and credit/source for every location photograph, drone clip, museum asset, 3D model and map; a public source link does not itself license imagery.
- Respect sacred-site visiting norms and rules. Specific claims about visiting Al-Hijr require review of related Sahih hadith rather than casual tourism framing; do not instruct viewers to violate restrictions.
- For uncertain site identification, prefer neutral maps with an uncertainty note, not precise pins implying certainty.
