# JOTA Arabic Learning — MSA preset v1.3.0

**Status:** teaching design only, not authorized video generation.

## Purpose
Teach Indonesian children/family viewers useful Arabic with meaningful context and accurate pronunciation, while keeping all human and animal characters 100% faceless. Set learning language **Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى)** by default. Regional dialects are separate deliberate lessons and must be labeled accurately. Grade/age target is set in each Lesson Brief.

## Default episode contract
- 9:16 YouTube Shorts planned 40–75 seconds *or* a separate approved landscape lesson; duration is a creative target, not a platform promise.
- One short lesson objective, normally 2–4 vocabulary items or one functional dialogue and a closing recall question. Do not mix multiple grammar rules into a very short episode.
- Explanation primarily in Indonesian, example speech in Arabic from a validated natural voice. Distinguish phonemes carefully: ح vs ه, ع vs ء, ق vs ك, ث vs س, ذ vs ز, ص vs س etc. Accuracy of *every* target pronunciation must be checked with a proficient speaker.
- Characters from JOTA-approved Bible. For dialogue, show speaker via shot blocking, clothes, fixed color/role labels and captions; **never lip-sync or draw a mouth**, even when speech is heard.
- No songs or background music. Small environmental sound effects are optional and must not mask speech.

## Orthography/typography rules
- All Arabic visible words and sentences are rendered **after** generation as editable/typeset overlays using Arabic-capable fonts and genuine right-to-left shaping/ordering. Never rely on AI-generated lettering inside background images.
- Store each approved Arabic sentence as Unicode source text; optional accurate harakat (tashkil) when appropriate to beginners, Indonesian translation, pronunciation note and transliteration (as secondary learning aid only; avoid falsely suggesting transliteration is fully faithful).
- Mixed Latin + Arabic lines require actual bidirectional text layout and visual verification of word/punctuation order, ligatures, punctuation, numeral placement, line breaks and safe margins on mobile.
- Native-script labels must not be mirrored, reversed, truncated, respelled or inferred from generated imagery.
- Qur'anic verses, hadith or devotional formulas require traceable primary references and separate religious review. **Do not turn AI-generated voice into purported Qur'an recitation** or falsely attribute invented lines to revelation.

## Suggested didactic sequence per video
1. Hook in Indonesian with everyday situation.
2. On-screen one approved Arabic utterance + Indonesian meaning, pronounced clearly once.
3. Animate one simple scene that physically illustrates the meaning without facial features.
4. Repeat phrase slowly once, then at normal pace if audio quality supports it.
5. Ask viewer a short retrieval/choice question; leave an actual pause to think.
6. Show exact answer text and encourage practice; no invented religious attribution.

## Example content conventions
- `السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ` / `وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ` are culturally/religiously familiar greetings; any specific claims about spiritual reward need a properly verified source, not just this exercise.
- `كِتَابٌ` (book), `قَلَمٌ` (pen), `مَدْرَسَةٌ` (school): sample beginner **nominal forms with final tanwin**; sentences change ending with syntax, so do not pretend these case endings are invariant.
- Never assert every Arabic teaching app/voice engine correctly pronounces vowels, emphatics or hamza. Test first.

## Content review
Arabic-script/translation reviewer, pronunciation reviewer, visual QA reviewer and owner are distinct check results even if one person fills multiple roles. Default status NOT_RUN. If reliable speaker/RTL pipeline is unavailable, produce storyboard/approved script only and mark MEDIA_BLOCKED.
