# Example — JOTA original Islamic inspiration mini-story (PLAN ONLY)

**Episode idea:** “Payung untuk Tetangga” — *fictional* Indonesian village story exploring `ihsan` and kindness. This is a demonstration of the new skill's modular documentation, not a generation request or a claim about a real religious narration.

## Story concept
During a rainstorm, a child sees an elderly neighbor carrying groceries without an umbrella. The child returns home briefly, brings an umbrella and walks the neighbor to shelter. The parent later acknowledges the considerate act. The narrator reflects on everyday kindness without attributing an invented quote to the Qur'an, the Prophet, or a historical person.

## Character bible (fictionalized aliases)
- `JOTA-KID-01`: Indonesian primary-school child, modest sage outerwear and light kufi, shorter silhouette, **100% blank face**; emotions through hands/head tilt/body position only.
- `JOTA-NEIGHBOR-01`: elderly neighbor, cream loose outerwear and dark wrap, gently stooped stance, **100% blank face**.
- `JOTA-PARENT-01`: adult guardian in terracotta long sleeves, back view, **100% blank face**.
- Props: a teal umbrella, paper grocery bag, bamboo gate and tiled village walkway. Colors/shapes never change.

## Example prompt — SC03 start image
```
SC03-IMG-V1
Style: original Indonesian 2D painterly storybook; muted green and cream.
Only hands and sleeves of JOTA-KID-01 enter the frame, taking a folded
teal umbrella from a bamboo wall hook. Camera mid-close, overcast window
light, quiet tiled entryway. Crop above hands; no heads, faces, animals,
text, logos, reflections or music-bearing devices.
```

## Example motion direction
```
SC03-MOTION-V1
Single continuous gentle close-up: a child reaches with one hand,
grasps the folded teal umbrella, lifts it from the hook and exits right.
Locked camera, natural sleeve motion and distinct beginning/end positions.
Audio: roof rain and wooden hook creak. No speech, no music, no face.
```
