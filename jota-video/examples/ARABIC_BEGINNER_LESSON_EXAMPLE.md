# Example — JOTA Arabic Story Lesson 01: Di Mana Bukunya? (PLAN ONLY)

**Illustrative lesson design, not verified classroom content or a production order.** Target: Indonesian beginner children, Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى). Visual style: JOTA original 2D cinematic storybook; no music; all characters/animals completely faceless; off-screen/voiceover speech, no lip-sync.

## Learning objective
By the end, learners can **recognize and practice** asking the location of a book and answering that it is on a table, with intelligible pronunciation.

## Teaching lines — draft for fluent Arabic-language review
- **Arabic question:** `أَيْنَ الْكِتَابُ؟` — Indonesian: “Di mana bukunya?”
- **Arabic response:** `الْكِتَابُ عَلَى الطَّاوِلَةِ.` — Indonesian: “Bukunya ada di atas meja.”
- An Indonesian narrator explains the meaning. A vetted Arabic voice says each line naturally; no character moves its lips. The markings and pronunciation must be reviewed by a qualified speaker before release.
- Scope excludes making up religious sayings. This is ordinary language practice, not quoted scripture.

## Fictional characters and reusable props
- `LEARNER-A`: faceless child, navy long-sleeve clothing, cream peci, small frame, used mostly from behind.
- `LEARNER-B`: faceless older sibling, sage-green modest outfit, white peci. Hands gesturing or turning toward a table; **no facial marks from any camera angle**.
- Book: blue cover, exact matching shape and thickness; table: warm wood, same position in all shots. No printed words on generated book cover.

## Suggested beat list (not a render schedule)
| Beat | Objective/action | Visual perspective | Sound/typography | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B01 | Curiosity: child looks around for a blue book | Rear medium shot, village room | Indonesian narrator asks where the book is | No facial details |
| B02 | Learner-A looks at older sibling | Hands/rear angle | Approved Arabic question, off-screen voice; add correct RTL overlay | Check Arabic script and phonemes |
| B03 | Older sibling points at blue book on table | Hands and prop close-up | Approved Arabic answer, off-screen voice with Indonesian translation | Prop position and case endings |
| B04 | Viewers repeat and identify the book | Book close-up with room for text | Repetition, short silent gap with ambient sound, not music | Reading time and deliberate pause |
| B05 | Answer reveal/feedback | Same table, original simple camera movement | Arabic line on deterministic typeset overlay | Check text not AI-generated |

## QA examples
- **Reject** any shot with eyes/nose/mouth, including drawn faces on toys/animals.
- **Reject** reversed or detached Arabic glyphs; store captions as Unicode and render RTL with an Arabic-capable text engine.
- **Reject** Arabic voice if `ط` in `الطَّاوِلَةِ` is wrongly pronounced; require fluent review (this is a test prompt, not an assertion about a specific voice).
- **Reject** an output that looks like five photos fading: motion must visibly support the story and teaching action.
- **Block** generation unless user explicitly orders it and the chosen operator (AI with browser control, or Manual) and existing quality gates permit.
