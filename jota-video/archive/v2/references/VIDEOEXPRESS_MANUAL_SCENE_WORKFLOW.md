## Seven authoritative owner steps — preserve order

1. **SCENE 01 ONLY — IMAGE PROMPT = character + action + farm background.** Write precisely ONE initial Image Prompt using the approved character/reference identity, current pose and expression, foreground/environment, visible ducks/props and their positions, weather/light and established art style. It is a **still-image composition**, not a spoken or moving-camera prompt. **For SC02 onward, skip the Image Prompt field entirely** (even if visible in the UI). Never author scene-specific Image Prompts or propose fresh still/image generation for those continuations; the saved previously rendered seam frame supplies the image.
2. **SCENE 01 reference and still controls ONLY.** Select the exact approved existing character image in **Reference Photo 1**, set **Image Type: Human** for a human reference where appropriate, and enable **Use Creative mode**, **Automatically enhance my image prompt** and **Use Consistent Character**. Manually create/check the SC01 still once; confirm unchanged face and black hijab when required. **From SC02 onward, select the exact saved seam frame in Use from Library instead of creating another image**. Reuse the original character identity as reference only if the observed VIDEO flow offers a compatible identity control, with no new Image Prompt/Create Image. Preserve the visible settings when applicable but do not mistake a still-generation toggle for an instruction to generate more stills.
3. **SC01–SCN VIDEO AND AUDIO PROMPT = shoot + physical action + verbatim speech + sounds.** Every scene receives this prompt, starting the first with the scene's entry action and subsequent prompts with continuity from the **previous selected frame**, never from an imagined newly generated image. Include professional scenario-appropriate shot style (for selfie, `A wide-angle selfie shows ...`), camera height/lens, direction/velocity and framing, character's action and gaze, relevant ducks/props, lighting/weather, `ADEGAN`, **`SCRIPT NGOMONG`** with exact short Indonesian speech, voice/performance cues, and appropriate duck/footstep/wind/water/bird sounds. Keep speech separate under the explicit label and do not let enhancement rewrite it.
4. **SC01–SCN VIDEO SETTINGS.** Advanced Mode ON, Enhance Video Prompt ON (inspect exact dialogue), Manual 10-second target, **Video Only OFF for speaking characters**, Public Gallery OFF. Never replace speech with standalone TTS.
5. **GENERATE VIDEO AND SPEAKING VOICE TOGETHER, NOT ANOTHER IMAGE.** In the same VideoExpress Create Video call use the scene's Video and Audio Prompt with verbatim SCRIPT NGOMONG. For SC02+ choose the previously saved native end frame first. Do not open standalone Text to Speech to create character dialogue. Generate optional ambient/Foley separately only.
6. **CONTINUE SC02–SCN FROM SELECTED NATIVE VIDEO ENDING IMAGE — NO IMAGE PROMPT.** Inspect the actual preceding rendered footage and choose the clearest, most stable image in its ending, with face/black hijab, props, ducks/background and camera direction intact; it may be slightly earlier than the raw final frame. Save that exact frame in VideoExpress's image library, **trim the preceding clip at precisely that saved frame** and verify that the post-trim final displayed image matches. Start the next video by **selecting that same saved image through `Use from Library`** as its first image/input; do not write/fill `Image Prompt` and do not click `Create Image` for the next scene. Write its **Video and Audio Prompt ONLY**, with the camera already mid-action and dialogue/ambient sounds continuing naturally. Repeat video generation → frame selection → trim → saved-frame reuse → next video prompt for every subsequent scene. If the selected native image cannot be passed as the exact next first frame, mark `CONTINUITY_SOURCE_BLOCKED`; don't generate a lookalike and claim it is the same frame.
7. **DIRECTOR-LEVEL CAMERA CONTINUITY.** Every shot must have a motivated professional camera decision based on **what is happening**; do not mechanically force all videos to selfie. For **selfie/influencer recording**, use the owner's preferred opening `A wide-angle selfie shows ...`; medium close-up with front-facing phone-camera perspective; **the influencer/primary character remains visible in the frame the ENTIRE time**, with the same hijab/outfit when locked; the **smartphone and filming arm/device remain out of view** if requested. Preserve front-camera distance, camera height, walking direction and natural handheld micro-motion across continuation clips. For a non-selfie shot, choose an appropriate wide/medium/detail/tracking/pan/dolly and apply actor framing only as needed by the storyline. Avoid arbitrary zooms, reverse-axis jumps, hard reframe, teleporting or accidental cuts. Keep nature/ambience continuous. Check actual **moving** ~0.5–1s before and after seams plus dialogue/audio; matching static frames or adjacent timeline tiles alone do NOT prove a smooth take.

## Mandatory verification before labeling a manual pilot GOOD

- `IMAGE_APPROVED`: SC01 character/image verified; for SC02 onward **NO NEW IMAGE GENERATION**, same saved selected previous end-frame asset ID used as video first-frame source. If unavailable, `CONTINUITY_SOURCE_BLOCKED`.
- `SHOT_READY`: camera style and character-in-frame rule relevant to the **actual** shot, spoken line fits measured clip duration, atmosphere and props consistent.
- `PROVIDER_JOB_VERIFIED`: actual VideoExpress job/clip exists and runs; public gallery OFF.
- `BOUNDARY_READY`: selected clean native frame saved, predecessor trimmed to it, next frame/link checked. Reject `UNTRIMMED_PREDECESSOR`, `REFERENCE_CHANGED`, `CAMERA_JUMP`, `UNVERIFIED_FIRST_FRAME`.
- `AUDIO_QA`: listen and watch the same native video+speaking-audio output for exact Indonesian words and voice, plus mode-specific face rule (JOTA 1 no facial features; JOTA 2 natural mouth sync), and ambient sound. Background/Foley TTS may be separately generated; *character* TTS cannot replace or duplicate same-job dialogue. Neither prompt nor metadata proves audiovisual synchronization.
- `FULL_TAKE_QA`: review all real joined moving footage/audio. `NOT_RUN` is not PASS.
