## New contract: speech is the performance master

1. **Speaker-first storyboard**: one speaking character and one exact Indonesian line per dialogue shot/turn; camera front/three-quarter muzzle closeup (enough facial pixels), conversational eye contact, mouth unobstructed. Listener reacts silently, mouth at rest; alternate shot/reverse-shot. Physical-action wides may remain silent/cutaway, but never portray visible talking with unlinked track.
2. **Natural dialogue before media**: write as genuine *spoken* Bahasa Indonesia, not stiff narrator prose; brief contemporary vocabulary, clear intent/subtext, dramatic turn-taking, proper character voice contrasts, natural pauses, reaction time, short interruptions only if provider supports overlapping voices. Read the script aloud/preview the speech duration and adjust text **before** any expensive lipsync render; preserve punctuation and emotional stress. Avoid making all lines same cadence. Stage direction is a separate field, never part of TTS input.
3. **Master audio / voice identity**: Use VideoExpress's native supported character speech/lipsync generation path; when a reusable native audio asset can be explicitly selected, it becomes authoritative `speech_audio_asset_id`, speech content and duration and should drive actual mouth movement. If native VideoExpress `Lipsync HD Video` instead generates speech + moving mouth jointly from a specific `Video and Audio Prompt`, the one native combined output becomes authoritative. Either route is valid **only after a real 2D animal test succeeds**. Actor voice ID/style is reused as closely as actual UI permits. "Choose my audio" / "Narration Video" may supply audio but is NOT presumed lipsync; check actual controls.
4. **P3S_SYNC_READY before provider job**: exact `artistly_reference_id`, `ve_imported_image_id`, `speaker_character_id`, audible literal line, emotional/voice instructions, 16:9 both editor/generator, JOTA 2 2D, facial framing/muzzle anatomy, expected `speech_start/end`, 0.3–1s silence/setup and closing-rest reaction margin where feasible, verified choice `LIPSYNC_HD_COMBINED` or `LIPSYNC_AUDIO_COUPLED`, private output and reasonable quota/cost. **NEVER enable `video_only` for an on-camera speaking shot.** Neither random mouth action nor "add speech later" is allowed.
5. **Native generation**: Create once with verified speaker image/audio/route. Record exact voice ID, source still, chosen controls, audio prompt, VideoExpress UUID/clip. If an integrated prompt route supplies audio and lipsync together, DO NOT add the same voice again on a separate track. If audio-coupled route uses pre-recorded source, do not also generate second speech layer. Preserve raw clip and don't double-submit pending jobs.
6. **Actual sync-and-naturalness pilot**: Before rendering a full new film, run **one** low-cost **single speaking actor, one natural short line** from the same Kancil/Buaya cast in VideoExpress. View and **listen** to actual sample at normal speed with mouth clearly visible (first/mid/last frames **cannot** prove lip-sync). Compare syllabic opening/closure to sound onset and pauses, no mouth during silence, no second actor talking, line fidelity, natural intonation/accent, facial stability. If missing real audio in generated output or speech not in Indonesian, fail pilot and investigate different **native VideoExpress** path. Do not claim success from button visibility or waveforms.
7. **PASS/FAIL:** `DIALOGUE_LIPSYNC_PASS` needs actual audible listening AND visible mouth timing plus exact words. `VOICE_NATURAL_PASS` needs human/audibly validated tone/rhythm/emotional context, not an opinion from metadata. `LINE_FIDELITY_PASS` checks exact Indonesian line. `CHARACTER_TURN_PASS` verifies only the speaking character articulates. `SILENCE_BEHAVIOR_PASS`: muzzle rests during non-speech. `TECHNICAL_PASS`: native video, true 16:9, character stable, no hallucinated titles/extra subjects. Missing any evidence -> `PILOT_QA_PENDING` not PASS. Use minor bounded tune/revision; no automatic batch retries.
8. **Scale only after successful pilot**: version-lock proven HD route, speaking cast and voice prompt master in production manifest, then draft 8–10 dialogue shot cards, short silent reaction/crossing inserts, and batch compatible Artistly frames. Each speaking shot uses same recipe with character-specific voice descriptions and verified provider controls. Audio and facial performance always produced together or native-source-coupled.
9. **Final film**: one private VideoExpress native export; review complete **audiovisual playback** (human listening and looking together), turning points, onscreen speaker, dialogue fidelity, volume consistency, no narrator, optional rights-cleared music, transitions/physical story and all major character anatomy. Visual-only frame samples and audio-only RMS independently are **insufficient** for final dialog QC. Owner approves before publishing.
